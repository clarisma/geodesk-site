[GeoDesk for Python](https://docs.geodesk.com/python) Version 2.2 introduces a series of spatial operations: `area()`, `buffer()`, `distance()` and `length()`. We've also made it easier to query relation members that have specific roles (`Features.with_role()`).

Install / upgrade: `pip install geodesk -U`

([Release notes](https://github.com/clarisma/geodesk-py/releases/tag/v2.2.0))
